| T-06-03-03 | DoS | A malformed env value (`THEORETICAL_EQUITY_USD=abc`) crashes startup | accept | pydantic-settings raises `ValidationError` at app startup, not at request time. `docker compose up` will fail loudly — operator-visible, no runtime crash. |
